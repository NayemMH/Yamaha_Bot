================================================================================
YAMAHA MOTORBIKE BD AI ASSISTANT - PRODUCT IDEA & OVERVIEW
================================================================================

1. Name of your AI-Based Product Idea
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Yamaha Motorbike BD AI Assistant
(Yamaha Bangladesh AI Chatbot & Service Assistant for ACI Motors)


2. Briefly describe your idea and what business/function impact it will bring.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PRODUCT DESCRIPTION:
The Yamaha Motorbike BD AI Assistant is an end-to-end, omni-channel AI sales
consultant and service diagnostic platform built specifically for Yamaha Bangladesh 
(ACI Motors). Powered by Google Gemini (with built-in offline fallback pathways) 
and integrated directly into Web and WhatsApp, it serves as an interactive 24/7 
virtual assistant for prospective motorcycle buyers and existing riders.

Key Functional Capabilities:
- Smart Bike Recommender & Dynamic Upselling: Interactive quiz matching rider 
  budget, riding purpose, and features (e.g., ABS, mileage) with AI-generated 
  persuasive sales pitches and smart model upsells.
- AI Audio Engine Sound Analyzer: Uses Gemini's multimodal audio processing to 
  analyze recorded or uploaded engine sounds, diagnosing specific mechanical 
  issues (e.g., timing chain friction, tappet noise, piston slap).
- Service Diagnostic Knowledge Base: Matches rider-reported symptoms against a 
  Knowledge Base (~30 issues) detailing root cause, urgency, and specific 
  recommended products/lubricants (Yamalube, CEAT tires, Liqui Moly).
- Localized Location Picker & Auto-Correction: Integrates all 64 Bangladesh 
  districts & upazilas with fuzzy logic matching (e.g., auto-correcting "Sylet" 
  to Sylhet) for accurate territory routing.
- Automated Lead & Service Dispatch: Automated dual-channel dispatch via Gmail 
  SMTP Email and WhatsApp (whatsapp-web.js) forwarding leads and service 
  bookings directly to territory managers and technicians.


BUSINESS & FUNCTIONAL IMPACT:

1. Accelerated Sales Conversion & AOV Growth:
   Dynamic budget upselling and tailored product recommendations guide customers 
   toward higher-tier Yamaha models (e.g., R15, FZS) and official accessories/
   lubricants, driving higher average order value (AOV).

2. 24/7 Omni-Channel Customer Support:
   Provides instant bilingual (Bengali & English) consultation on both web and 
   WhatsApp without manual staff intervention, capturing leads around the clock.

3. Faster Service Triage & Reduced Diagnostic Time:
   The audio sound analyzer and diagnostic KB pre-identify bike issues before 
   the customer arrives at a service center, allowing technicians to prepare 
   parts in advance and speed up turn-around times.

4. Zero-Friction Territory Lead Routing:
   Automated Bangladesh district/upazila typo correction ensures leads and 
   appointment requests are routed to the exact regional dealer or technician 
   without manual sorting.

5. Automated Lead Logistics & Higher Retention:
   Real-time dual dispatch (Email + direct WhatsApp message) eliminates lost 
   leads, boosts appointment show-up rates, and deepens post-purchase customer 
   loyalty.
